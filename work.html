Principle 01
Never restate what's already visible.

Every caption has to add something the viewer can't already see or hear. If the reel shows it, the caption earns its space by going deeper.

Principle 02
Voice before vocabulary.

Each client speaks differently. Sarah is confident and direct. Shreya leads with uncomfortable truths. The writing follows the person, not a template.

Principle 03
Short with intent. Not short by default.

The goal is precision. Every word either earns its place or gets cut. The hook is set first and never changed; everything else gets edited around it.
